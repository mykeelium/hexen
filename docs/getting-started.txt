Welcome to Hexen
================

This guide opens in a terminal. Use the arrow keys to scroll; press q to close.
Super is the key usually marked with a Windows logo on a PC keyboard.

Start using your desktop
------------------------
Super+Space          Find and open an application.
Super+Enter          Open a terminal.
Super+Shift+B        Open your browser.
Super+Shift+F        Open the file manager.
Super+W              Close the focused window.
Super+1 through 0    Switch workspace.
Super+Shift+1 to 0   Move the focused window to a workspace.
Super+Ctrl+L         Lock your session.
Super+Escape         Lock, suspend, log out, restart or shut down.
Super+Alt+Space      Open the Hexen menu.

In the Hexen menu, Network connects to Wi-Fi, Audio selects sound devices,
Bluetooth manages paired devices, and Review updates opens the update review.
Diagnostics displays a local, read-only system report; press q to close it.
The leftmost icon in the top bar also opens the Hexen menu. Super+K opens your
local Hyprland settings. See docs/desktop.md in your Hexen checkout for more.

If the graphical login or desktop does not work
------------------------------------------------
Press Ctrl+Alt+F2 to try another text console, then sign in with your account.
The desktop and recovery guides in your Hexen checkout describe the next steps:
docs/desktop.md and docs/recovery.md. To inspect the system from a terminal,
run: hexen doctor

Getting help
------------
See docs/quickstart.md in the Hexen checkout for reporting instructions. Share
the version, failed stage and relevant error; review logs before sharing and
remove passwords, tokens, private keys and recovery material.
